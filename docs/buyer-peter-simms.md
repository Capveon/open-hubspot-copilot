# Example buyer script — Peter Simms

Roleplay tape for the built-in test queue. Read it out loud as the prospect. You are not the rep. Hang up between paths. One path per call.

This is Capveon’s example motion (water ops). Fork it for your own buyers.

**Who you are:** Peter Simms, water ops superintendent, Gainesville Regional Utilities. You run crews. Engineering owns the CIP. You did not ask for this call.

**Queue:** GRU — water ops (test). Overlay the number with `OHC_TEST_PHONE`.

Finn clicks **Delivered** after the opener. You wait until he finishes a line, then read the next one. Do not stack two of your lines in one breath unless the script says so.

Glass fails if it repeats the product (three-time breaker, year installed, rank the CIP) after you already moved on. It passes if the next line uses something you just said.

---

## Path A — default (run this first)

Pickup.

> This is Peter, who's this?

Wait through his opener.

> Sure. Yeah, go for it.

Wait.

> Yeah, can you tell me a little bit more? Right now at our yearly CIP our engineers go through and do this process.

Wait. He should ask about the yearly meeting or who ranks it. Not re-pitch.

> Yeah. I might not be the right person for this. That's really an engineering thing on our side.

Wait. He should ask who to talk to. Then you can hang up, or give him a name:

> You'd want Maria in engineering. She runs that list.

Then hang up.

---

## Path B — what do you do

Pickup.

> This is Peter, who's this?

Wait through the opener.

> Go for it. What is it you guys actually do?

Wait. One explanation of the gap is fine here.

> Okay. Our engineers already do a lot of that.

Wait. He should stay on engineers, not recast the pitch.

Hang up whenever you have seen enough.

---

## Path C — we have Cityworks

Pickup, then after the opener:

> Yeah, twenty seconds.

Wait.

> We already have Cityworks. And GIS.

Wait. He should ask where it stops helping you **rank**, not offer to replace it.

> Yeah, Cityworks is the work orders. The CIP is still a spreadsheet planning rebuilds every spring.

Hang up.

---

## Path D — no time

Pickup, then after the opener:

> I don't really have time.

Wait. One question, then he should offer to go.

> Make it fast.

Wait one more line, then:

> Send me an email. I gotta jump.

Hang up.

---

## Path E — interested

Pickup, then after the opener:

> Sure, go ahead.

Wait through whatever he says.

> Yeah, that actually is a headache for us.

Wait. He should go for a time or a zone, not explain the product again.

> Thursday afternoon's okay. East side of town.

Hang up.

---

## Voicemail (optional)

Let it ring to box, or as soon as you pick up:

> Hi, you've reached Peter Simms with Gainesville Regional Utilities. Please leave a message.

Glass should say hang up. Don't leave a pitch.

---

## Notes

Stay Peter. You run crews, not the CIP packet. You can be friendly. You do not buy software on this call.

If you go off script, that is still a valid test. Say one new fact and see if glass answers **that** fact.
